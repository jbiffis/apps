Scoring Logic

Handicap (initial): Sum of gross scores across 2 practice rounds − total par for those rounds.

Points per round: Rank 8 players by net score (gross − handicap). Best = 8 pts, worst = 1 pt. Ties split the tied positions' total points evenly. Absent players share the worst position.

Golf score formatting: Single circle = birdie, double circle = eagle, no mark = par, single square = bogey, double square = double bogey, solid square = 3+ over par.

Closest to pin: Hole + yardage recorded per round. Players enter distances. Winner = shortest distance. If no one made the green, $20 rolls to next round.

Chip-in pot: Everyone pays $2/round into a pot. When a chip-in is recorded, the current pot is awarded.
